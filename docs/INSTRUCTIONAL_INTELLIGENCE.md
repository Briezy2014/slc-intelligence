# Instructional intelligence

**Status:** Active assistive toolkit  
**Routes:** `/instructional-intelligence`, `/para-supports`

## Purpose

Make SLC Intelligence useful for instruction and team preparation—not only compliance paperwork—while keeping humans in charge of educational decisions.

## Tools

1. Present levels draft from pasted evidence
2. Goal–need matching / coverage gaps
3. Measurable goal flagging
4. ETR / IEP / progress consistency checks (drafting aid)
5. Parent-friendly technical summaries
6. Instructional plans from IEP goals
7. Para-friendly approved supports explainer
8. Meeting preparation summaries

## Related live modules

- Intervention fidelity and progress: `/interventions`
- Missing/overdue data signals: `/command-center`, `/administrative-intelligence`
- Human-reviewed AI drafting: `/ai-assist`
- Family communication + read acknowledgment: `/family-communication`

## Guardrails

- Educator review required before saving/sending
- No eligibility, placement, or diagnosis determinations
- Consistency/admin views are not legal compliance certifications
- Prefer coded/de-identified student context during pilots
