# Pilot de-identified use rules — SLC Intelligence

**Status:** Active operational rule for classroom pilot  
**Audience:** Classroom staff using SLC Intelligence before district approval for identifiable student data  
**Not legal advice:** District/counsel review still governs any later use of identifiable student information.

---

## Pilot rule (summary)

For now, we are **only** using SLC Intelligence with **coded / de-identified practice data**.  
Do **not** enter student names or other information that could identify a student.

If you need to refer to a student, use a classroom code such as **S1, S2, S3**.  
Keep any private key that links codes to real students **outside this system** (for example, a paper roster only authorized classroom staff can see). Do **not** type that key into SLC Intelligence, AI Assist, notes, uploads, or communications.

Until the district reviews SLC Intelligence and, if appropriate, approves identifiable use, follow district procedures before entering any real student PII. Until then, use the platform as a **classroom productivity and planning tool** with coded or sample data only.

---

## What we CAN use it for during the pilot

- Practice collecting classroom data
- Behavior tracking using student codes (example: S1, S2, S3)
- Goal and objective practice
- Data collection on prompts, independence, accuracy, and participation
- ABC behavior observations using student codes
- Daily notes using non-identifying information
- Task analysis and skill progression
- AI lesson ideas and instructional supports (with coded/sample context only)
- Classroom planning
- Visual schedules and activity planning
- Practicing reports using sample or coded data
- Practicing structured draft forms with **fictional / sample** content only

---

## What we CANNOT enter

- Student names or initials
- Birthdates
- Real student ID / SIS numbers (use classroom codes like S1 instead of district IDs)
- Real IEPs or Evaluation Team Reports (ETRs), including uploads/scans/photos of those documents
- Medical information tied to a real student
- Parent/guardian names or contact information
- Addresses
- Photos of students
- Any document containing personally identifiable student information (PII)
- Free-text descriptions that would let someone outside the classroom identify the student  
  (for example, unique family details, exact home location, or rare identifying circumstances)
- Real family communication content that names a student or family member

---

## Practical reminders

1. **Local identifier / student name fields:** enter codes such as `S1` / `Student S1`, not real names or SIS IDs.
2. **Education documents / OCR upload:** do not upload real district IEPs/ETRs during the pilot.
3. **AI Assist:** do not paste identifiable student or family information into prompts.
4. **Family Communication contacts:** do not enter real parent names, emails, or phone numbers during the pilot.
5. **If unsure whether something is identifying — leave it out.**

---

## After district approval

Once the district has reviewed SLC Intelligence and, if appropriate, approves its use with identifiable student information, staff will follow whatever procedures the district requires (agreements, training, access controls, and counsel-approved privacy terms) before entering real student PII.
