# Capability roadmap

**Status:** Active product communication  
**Date:** 2026-07-30

## Positioning

SLC Intelligence is built to be **instructionally useful**, not only a compliance-document system. Drafts and flags support educators; humans retain decision-making authority.

## Active special education classroom capabilities

- Daily data collection
- Goal tracking
- Behavior Detective
- AI lesson planning
- Progress monitoring
- Student profiles
- AI draft IEP goals
- Accommodation tracker
- Parent communication log
- Ohio-aligned blank IEP / ETR / progress report drafts
- Signature workflows (receipt acknowledgment)
- Parent share packets
- Deadline / timeline tracker (reminders, not legal determinations)
- Instructional intelligence toolkit (present levels, goal–need match, measurability flags, consistency checks, parent-friendly summaries, instructional plans from goals, para supports, meeting prep)
- Differentiated instructional packets (30–100 pages from learner profiles; Easy/Moderate/Challenging/Errorless/Task analysis/ABA/UDL)

## Benefits emphasized for special education teams

- Time savings
- Better documentation
- More consistent progress monitoring
- AI assistance (educator-reviewed)
- Parent communication
- Compliance reminders (timeline organization only)
- Instructional usefulness beyond paperwork

## Future capabilities — not activated

These remain gated until district and legal counsel approve:

- SIS integration
- EMIS support
- Expanded Section 504 module
- Compliance reports
- OCR reporting packages
- State reporting

## Honesty / guardrails

- Ohio-aligned blanks are structured drafting aids, not official ODE fillable legal PDFs.
- Consistency checks and admin dashboards are workflow/readiness aids, not automated legal compliance determinations.
- The platform does not claim FERPA/IDEA certification.
- Human IEP team authority remains required for educational decisions.
